How to write an Advantages & Disadvantages essay

You get a topic with three points. Write about the good side, the bad side, and give your own advice. Use the tabs below, then write your own essay.

What you must do

1. AdvantagesWrite the GOOD points of the topic. Explain them.
2. DisadvantagesWrite the BAD points of the topic. Explain them.
3. RecommendationGive your own ADVICE or SUGGESTION.

Length: 80–110 words (at least 80; more is fine)

Four paragraphs

1. Introduction

Write about the topic in your own words. Say what you will write about.
Nowadays, … · These days, … · Many people … · In this essay, I will write about the advantages and disadvantages of …

2. Advantages

Give 1–2 good points. Explain them and add an example.
Firstly, … · One advantage is that … · Another advantage is … · For example, …

3. Disadvantages

Give 1–2 bad points. Explain them and add an example.
However, … · On the other hand, … · One disadvantage is that … · For instance, …

4. Conclusion

Say what you think and give your recommendation.
In conclusion, … · To sum up, … · I recommend that … · I suggest that … · In my opinion, …

Model essay: “Many people have online jobs.”

About 115 words. Read how each paragraph does its job, then write about your own topic.

Introduction
Nowadays, many people work online from home. In this essay, I will write about the advantages and disadvantages of having an online job and give my recommendation.
Advantages
One advantage is that you save time and money because you do not travel to an office. Another advantage is that you can plan your day. For example, a mother can work while her children are at school.
Disadvantages
However, there are some disadvantages. Firstly, you can feel lonely because you do not meet colleagues. Also, it is difficult to stop working, so people often work too late.
Conclusion + recommendation
In conclusion, an online job is good for many people. I recommend that workers make a daily plan and meet friends after work.

Avoid

  • Writing only advantages or only disadvantages.
  • Giving a point with no explanation or example.
  • Forgetting your recommendation.
  • Repeating the same linking word again and again.
  • Writing fewer than 80 words.
  • One long paragraph with no structure.

Do

  • Plan 4 paragraphs before you write.
  • Write about both sides: good and bad.
  • Explain each point and add an example.
  • Use linking words from the toolkit.
  • Check your spelling and word count.
  • End with a clear recommendation.
