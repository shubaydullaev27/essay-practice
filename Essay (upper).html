How to write an Agree & Disagree essay

You get a statement. React to it and support your opinion. Use the tabs below, then write your own essay.

What you must do

1. State your viewSay clearly if you AGREE or DISAGREE with the statement.
2. Support itGive detailed REASONS that explain and justify your opinion.
3. Illustrate itAdd an EXAMPLE and/or a SUGGESTION and/or a RECOMMENDATION.

Length: 120–150 words

Four paragraphs

1. Introduction

Paraphrase the given statement + write your position: agree or disagree.
Nowadays, … · It is often said that … · I (completely) agree / disagree with this statement because … · In my opinion, …

2. First body paragraph

Develop your 1st idea: topic sentence → explanation → example.
Firstly, … · One reason is that … · To begin with, … · For example, …

3. Second body paragraph

Develop your 2nd idea: topic sentence → explanation → example.
Secondly, … · Moreover, … · However, … · On the other hand, … · For instance, …

4. Conclusion

Restate your opinion in new words + your suggestions and recommendations.
In conclusion, … · To sum up, … · Overall, … · I recommend that … · I suggest that …

Six functions, six colours

Giving an opinion

  • I agree / disagree
  • In my opinion
  • I believe that
  • Personally,

Adding reasons

  • Firstly / Secondly
  • Moreover
  • In addition
  • Furthermore

Giving examples

  • For example
  • For instance
  • such as
  • A good example is

Showing contrast

  • However
  • On the other hand
  • Nevertheless
  • Despite this

Cause & effect

  • As a result
  • Therefore
  • This leads to
  • Consequently

Concluding

  • In conclusion
  • To sum up
  • Overall
  • I recommend that

Model essay: “Investing in education is very important.”

145 words. Read how each paragraph does its job, then write your own ideas on your own topic.

Introduction
Nowadays, many people believe that spending money on schools and universities is one of the best decisions a country can make. I completely agree with this statement because education gives young people better jobs and valuable knowledge.
Body 1: job opportunities
Firstly, education opens the door to better job opportunities. People with a good education can apply for well-paid positions and are less likely to be unemployed. For example, a graduate with strong English and computer skills can easily find work in an international company.
Body 2: knowledge acquisition
Secondly, education helps people to acquire knowledge. Students learn how to think critically, solve problems and understand the world around them. As a result, they make wiser decisions in their everyday lives.
Conclusion: recommendation
In conclusion, investing in education benefits both individuals and society. I recommend that young people study hard and use every opportunity to learn new skills, for instance by joining free courses online.

Avoid

  • Starting with your opinion only. Paraphrase the topic first.
  • Giving reasons with no explanation or example.
  • Forgetting an example, suggestion or recommendation.
  • Repeating the same linking word again and again.
  • Writing under 120 or over 150 words.
  • A conclusion that repeats the introduction word for word.

Do

  • Plan 4 paragraphs before you write.
  • One clear reason per body paragraph, then explain it, then give an example.
  • Vary your linking words with the toolkit.
  • Keep the same opinion from start to end.
  • Check your word count before sending.
  • End with a recommendation the reader can act on.
