Advantages & disadvantages essay

Learn the essay type, study the toolkit, analyse a band 9 sample, then write your own essay on the same topic.

What is it?

You discuss the good points (advantages) and the bad points (disadvantages) of a trend, change or situation.

Typical questions“What are the advantages and disadvantages of …?” · “Discuss the advantages and disadvantages …” · “… Give your own opinion.”
You mustCover BOTH sides, support each point with explanation and an example, and answer exactly what the question asks.
Opinion?Give your own opinion only if the question asks for it, or if you want a clear final judgement in the conclusion.

Length: at least 250 words

Four paragraphs

1. Introduction

Paraphrase the topic, say you will discuss both sides (and your view if asked).
Many … now … · This trend has benefits, but also drawbacks. · In this essay, I will examine both sides …

2. Advantages

Main advantage → explain → example. Add a second advantage if you can.
The main advantage is … · In addition, … · For example, …

3. Disadvantages

Main disadvantage → explain → example. Use contrast to move from advantages.
However, … · On the other hand, … · A further drawback is …

4. Conclusion

Sum up both sides and give a balanced view or your opinion.
Overall, … · On balance, … · I believe the benefits outweigh the drawbacks.

Language toolkit

Advantages (verbs)

  • improves / enhances / benefits
  • helps people (to) do
  • allows / enables people to do
  • offers / provides opportunities
  • boosts / creates / increases

Disadvantages (verbs)

  • worsens / exacerbates a problem
  • hinders / hampers / obstructs
  • prevents people from doing
  • deprives people of a chance
  • reduces / damages / destroys

Cause and result

  • leads to / results in
  • brings about / gives rise to
  • is responsible for
  • triggers (anxiety, unrest)
  • ensures that …

Linking words

  • In addition / Moreover / Furthermore
  • However / Nevertheless
  • On the other hand
  • While / Although …
  • For example / For instance

Sentence frames

  • One major advantage of … is that …
  • A further drawback is that …
  • … has a positive / negative impact ON …
  • This allows people to …

Concluding

  • Overall, …
  • On balance, …
  • In conclusion, …
  • The benefits outweigh the drawbacks.

Watch the grammar: impact ON · prevent people FROM doing · deprive people OF · allow people TO do. “Deteriorate” has no object: say the situation deteriorated.
