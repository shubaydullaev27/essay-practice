Essay Practice

Read the guide, choose a topic, write 100–150 words, then press Check my essay.

How to write your essay

You get a statement. Explain the idea, give reasons, add an example and some advice. Use the tabs below, then write your own essay.

What you must do

1. Explain the ideaAnswer the first point under your topic. Say why or how.
2. Give reasonsGive detailed REASONS that support your opinion. Explain each one clearly.
3. Add an example and adviceGive an EXAMPLE and/or a SUGGESTION and/or a RECOMMENDATION.

Length: 100–150 words

Three parts

1. Introduction (2–3 sentences)

Paraphrase the statement + give a general idea.
These days, … · Many people … · … has a big effect on people. · It is often said that …

2. Body

Reason 1: explain how or why. Reason 2: another reason, explained clearly. Example: a real-life situation that makes the idea clear.
Because of this, … · Another reason is that … · This is because … · For example, … · For instance, …

3. Advice / conclusion

Give advice or a recommendation. A question to the reader is very effective.
… should … · I recommend that … · It is a good idea to … · This simple step can help …

Useful language

Starting

  • These days, …
  • Nowadays, …
  • Many people …
  • It is often said that …

Giving reasons

  • Because of this, …
  • One reason is that …
  • This is because …
  • Another reason is …

Giving examples

  • For example, …
  • For instance, …
  • such as
  • A good example is …

Giving advice

  • People should …
  • I recommend that …
  • It is a good idea to …
  • They could try to …

Showing results

  • As a result, …
  • This helps people to …
  • so they can …
  • This leads to …

Model essay: “Advertising makes people buy things they don’t need.”

150 words. Notice how each paragraph does one job. Then write your own ideas on your own topic.

Introduction
Advertising has a big effect on people, and it often makes them buy things they do not really need.
Body: reason 1 (how ads influence people)
Companies use bright colors, happy music, and attractive people to make their products look exciting and important. Because of this, many people start to think that they must buy these products, even if their lives are already fine without them.
Body: reason 2
Advertising also works because people see it everywhere: on TV, on the internet, in the streets, and even in games. When we see the same message many times, we begin to believe that we should buy the product.
Body: example
For example, a teenager may already have good shoes, but after seeing many ads for a new brand, he suddenly wants those shoes too.
Advice
Young people should be careful when watching ads. They should ask themselves, “Do I really need this?” This simple question can help them make better decisions and save money.

Avoid

  • Copying the statement word for word in the introduction.
  • Giving a reason with no explanation.
  • Forgetting the example.
  • Forgetting advice or a recommendation at the end.
  • Repeating the same linking word again and again.
  • Writing under 100 or over 150 words.

Do

  • Plan before you write: introduction, body, advice.
  • Answer every point listed under your topic.
  • One idea per sentence, with clear linking words.
  • Use a real-life example.
  • End with advice the reader can use.
  • Check your word count before sending.
