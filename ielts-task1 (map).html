Maps: Describing Change

Study the map language, analyse the model report, then write your own report on the same maps.

Map report guide

Learn the task, study the map language, analyse a model report, then write your own report on the same maps.

What is it?

You are given two (or more) maps of the same place at different times. You describe what changed and what stayed the same. You do not give opinions or reasons.

Typical question“The maps show changes that took place in … over a … year period from … to ….”
You mustCompare the maps, group the changes, and write an overview that shows the main trend.
General main pointsMore urban, less rural, more developed, more modern.

Length: at least 150 words · about 20 minutes

Four paragraphs

1. Introduction

Paraphrase the question. Do not copy it.
The maps illustrate the developments which took place in … between … and … · The maps show the changes in …

2. Overview

Two main trends only. No small details.
Overall, a comparison of the two maps reveals a complete transformation from a largely rural to a mainly urban area.

3. Details 1

Describe the first group of changes (for example, the south).
In 1980, … but during the next 25 years … · The most noticeable change is that …

4. Details 2

Describe the second group of changes (for example, the north).
In contrast, … · Further developments were … · … was also constructed …

Language of direction

In the north

  • Inside an area
  • California is in the west of the United States.
  • Phu My Hung is in the south of HCM city.

To the north

  • Comparison / outside an area
  • China is to the north of Vietnam.
  • Cambodia is to the west of Vietnam.

In / to the southwest

  • Florida is in the southeast of the United States.
  • The entrance was moved to the southwest of the building.

Language of location

Be located / situated in

  • The shopping mall was located in the centre of the city and was knocked down to make way for a new university.

Opposite

  • A restaurant has been built on the opposite side of the road where the shop used to be.

Next to · Along · Across from

  • A new car park has been constructed next to the hotel.
  • There was a new sidewalk along the river.
  • The park is across from the school.

Grammar

Map grammar is simple: use the passive voice in the past tense (was built, were removed). Use the present perfect or future only if the maps show those times.

Buildings

  • demolished, knocked down
  • constructed, built, erected
  • The apartment was demolished.
  • The school was knocked down to make way for a car park.
  • The building was erected on the bank of the river.

Areas

  • A new residential area was built in place of the park.
  • A golf course was constructed to the west of the airport.
  • The park disappeared.
  • An airport appeared.

Trees

  • cut down / chopped down, removed, uprooted
  • All trees were cut down in the north of the river.
  • All the trees were removed and replaced by a factory.

Factories / facilities

  • established, installed, placed, put in
  • An airport was established.

Don’t say

A house was installed / established. Say: a house was built / constructed.

Areas and zones

Word pairs

  • urban area = a city
  • rural area = the countryside
  • industrial area = factories, manufacturing, processing
  • residential area = houses

Recreational area

  • Parks and other green areas where people do activities.

Entertainment area

  • Part of a commercial area. When you sing karaoke or watch a film at the theatre, the focus is money.

General main points: more urban, less rural, more developed, more modern. Use them in your overview.

How to use the model report

1. Read the maps, then the model report once. 2. Pick a colour, then select words in the report to highlight them. 3. Write 2–3 notes about why it is strong. 4. Write your own report under it.
